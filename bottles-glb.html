Akhmeteli · GLB + model-viewer

The bottles as 3D models

Real .glb assets loaded with Google's model-viewer — drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and tap View in your space on a phone for AR.

These are demo GLBs (low-poly, single material, no glass refraction). They prove the GLB-on-web pipeline + AR. For production we either refine these in Blender (real glass & print-ready labels) or keep the procedural viewer, which renders better glass today.
